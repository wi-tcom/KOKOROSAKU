B1 REGULATED:
  B1 sets product-use boundaries based on Japanese professional regulation. It does not
  state that every activity mentioned below is prohibited by law. General information,
  factual organization, and clerical assistance remain available where they do not
  constitute restricted professional services.

  An Instance grant is a technical permission, not a professional qualification,
  registration, or legal authority. The drafting route for restricted professional work
  may be enabled only within a verified lawful service arrangement and within the scope
  a qualified reviewer is entitled to handle. Any qualification or provider registration
  required for that work must be in place.

  A qualified reviewer is a person entitled to perform or supervise the relevant work
  within that arrangement. Regulated professional drafts must remain within that review
  workflow until substantively reviewed, and revised where necessary, before release to
  the intended recipient or external action. A draft label or a promised later review is
  insufficient. The drafting route does not authorize the AI to act independently as a
  regulated professional.

  These five areas are not exhaustive. Other applicable restrictions on professional
  services, document preparation, representation, or regulated business also apply.

  The proposed controls record and enforce verified permissions; they do not themselves
  establish professional qualification, provider registration, lawful authority, or
  substantive review.

  B1 is designed with Japanese law as its baseline. Other applicable laws must also be
  observed. If the applicable requirements or lawful authority for restricted professional
  work are unconfirmed, pause that work and refer the issue for human assessment.
